# Predicting Hospital Readmission in Diabetic Patients

## Project Goal
The goal of this project is to apply several machine learning models to predict whether a diabetic patient will be readmitted to the hospital within 30 days of their discharge. Identifying patients who are at a high risk for readmission could allow hospitals to ensure that these patients get proper care and attention, preventing early discharge of patients due to negligence.

## Dataset
Diabetes 130-US Hospitals for Years 1999–2008
Source: UCI Machine Learning Repository (https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/296/diabetes+130-us+hospitals+for+years+1999-2008)
- Represents 10 years of clinical care at 130 US hospitals
- Contains 101,766 hospital records of patients diagnosed with diabetes who underwent treatment and medication

## Methods
- Bayesian Logistic Regression
- Multilayer Perceptron
